# Notes for: Search the height in metres of the Eiffel Tower and of the Burj Khalifa. Then calculate (1) what percentage of the Burj Khalifa's height the Eiffel Tower is, and (2) how many Eiffel Towers stacked on top of each other would reach the Burj Khalifa's height.

## Round 1 - ask_search_llm

**Task:**
Find the official heights in metres of the Eiffel Tower and the Burj Khalifa. Provide reliable source URLs.

**Answer:**
**Official Heights (metres)**  

| Structure | Official Height (m) | Source |
|-----------|--------------------|--------|
| Eiffel Tower (Paris, France) – tip including antenna | **330 m** | Wikipedia – “Eiffel Tower” (height table shows “Tip 330 m”) – <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eiffel_Tower> |
| Burj Khalifa (Dubai, United Arab Emirates) – architectural top | **828 m** | PR Newswire press release announcing the tower’s official height – <https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/burj-khalifa-is-unveiled-to-the-world-and-its-official-height-is-828-metres-80657972.html> |

These values are the officially published heights used by the operators and developers of each structure.

## Round 2 - ask_maths_llm

**Task:**
Calculate the following with the given numbers, rounding results to two decimal places:

A. (330 / 828) * 100
B. 828 / 330

**Answer:**
**A. Percentage**

\[
\text{A}= \left(\frac{330}{828}\right)\times 100
\]

Calculation steps:  

1. Divide 330 by 828 → \( \frac{330}{828}=0.3985507246\)  
2. Multiply by 100 → \(0.3985507246 \times 100 = 39.8550724638\)

Rounded to two decimal places:  

\[
\boxed{39.86\%}
\]

---

**B. Ratio**

\[
\text{B}= \frac{828}{330}
\]

Calculation steps:  

1. Divide 828 by 330 → \( \frac{828}{330}=2.5090909091\)

Rounded to two decimal places:  

\[
\boxed{2.51}
\]
